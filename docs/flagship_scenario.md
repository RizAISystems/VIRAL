# Flagship Scenario

The public demonstration is one continuous synthetic race sequence.

## Phase 1 — conformant operation

Request, actuator effort, achieved state, correction channels and thermal response remain inside the synthetic expected relationship. No driver report is present and no action is justified.

## Phase 2 — early precursor

An early thermal-shaped non-conformance appears while the car remains operational and all hard limits remain unbroken.

The reasoner temporarily favors a developing thermal-management limitation. That conclusion is provisional.

## Phase 3 — control effort changes before performance output fails

The synthetic actuator effort required to maintain the requested air-charge state begins to rise.

At first, achieved boost still remains near target. The system therefore has evidence of changing vehicle behavior **before the achieved performance channel itself materially diverges**.

This is a central demonstration requirement.

## Phase 4 — new evidence changes the explanation

The earlier coolant signal begins to recover while actuator effort continues to rise. Requested-versus-achieved air-charge error then starts growing and intake/correction channels add supporting evidence.

The reasoner revises the preferred explanation to an air-charge / performance-control deviation.

The revision is part of the public decision trace.

## Phase 5 — projected margin loss

The vehicle remains operational and no hard threshold has been crossed, but the recent trend projects a critically narrow synthetic engineering margin.

The reasoner recommends preservation.

## Phase 6 — authority gate

The deterministic policy checks whether the configured authority permits the public synthetic action.

At A3, the system may apply only the simulator's bounded preservation state.

At A1, the same reasoning occurs but the action is blocked.

## Phase 7 — verification

The system continues observing the synthetic vehicle after the action.

The verifier determines whether margin is recovering. The action is not assumed successful merely because it was issued.

This closes the awareness → reasoning → authority → action → verification loop.
